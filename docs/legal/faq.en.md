# FAQ

**What do I get when I take the crown?**
Your name, avatar, message and link on the homepage until someone takes the crown from you, plus a permanent record of your reign, achievements and rank on your profile.

**Do I win anything?**
No. There are no prizes or payouts. You pay for visibility and glory.

**How long will I keep the crown?**
Nobody knows. Someone could take it in seconds, or you could reign for days. That's the game.

**Why did the price change?**
It rises with every takeover and drops every hour while nobody buys. See the Rules page for the exact numbers.

**What does "Someone is taking the crown" mean?**
Another player reserved the price and is paying. If they don't finish within a few minutes, the crown is available again.

**Can I get a refund?**
Purchases are final once you get the crown. You are refunded automatically only if the crown couldn't be delivered, for example if someone else took it while your payment was being confirmed.

**Do I need an account?**
No. You can pay with your email and sign in later with the same email to claim your profile.

**Why is my message not showing?**
It may be in a short review, or it may have been hidden for breaking the rules. The crown is still yours either way.

**How do I stop the emails?**
Every alert email has a link to turn it off, and you can manage all alerts in your profile settings.

**How do I delete my account?**
From your profile settings. Your past reigns stay in the history without your name or details.
